Bloom Dental Studio

An automated lead-to-consultation funnel for a dental clinic.

Client type
General and cosmetic dental clinic, Bonifacio Global City, Metro Manila
Project type
Workshop practice project (fictional training scenario, not a paid client engagement)
My role
Strategy, funnel design, automation blueprint, copy and build
Built
Landing page, calendar booking, pipeline and tags, show-up reminders, no-show recovery, whitening upsell, recall, review requests

The problem

Bloom Dental Studio, led by Dr. Chloe, is a busy clinic with a leaky front end. Four issues drove this project:

  • Leads slip away before booking. About 20 website leads arrive each month, but only about 6 book an appointment. That is a 30% rate.
  • Manual front desk. Scheduling, confirmation calls, reminders and no-show follow-ups are all handled by hand, with no automated system behind them.
  • High no-shows. 35% of booked patients don’t attend, and there is no recovery sequence to bring them back.
  • No post-consultation follow-up. Nothing exists to turn patients who complete a consult into Premium Whitening bookings.

The GoHighLevel account was a fresh build, with no existing pipelines, tags or workflows.

Walkthrough

A video tour of the funnel, from landing page to booked consultation, then the no-show recovery and whitening upsell.

The solution

I designed the funnel for the primary persona, Sarah, the “Busy Professional.” She is aged 28 to 45, lives or works in BGC or Makati, and is career-focused, image-conscious and short on time. Rather than asking for a big commitment, the funnel leads with a free 30-minute new-patient consultation. It backs the offer with the trust signals this audience looks for: before-and-after photos, Google reviews and visible credentials. Booking is built to need as little back-and-forth as possible.

Once booked, the patient moves through automatic confirmation and reminders. Patients who miss the appointment get a three-touch recovery sequence. Patients who attend get a Premium Whitening offer. The front desk no longer chases any of it by hand.

What I built

1. Landing page and funnel pages

A GHL landing page with the Bloom Dental New Patient Inquiry form above the fold, and a booking CTA for the Free 30-Min New Patient Consult calendar.

2. Pipeline

A pipeline named Bloom Dental Consultations tracks every lead from first inquiry through to an active patient: New Lead, Consult Booked, Consult Completed, Abandoned Consult, Upsell Booked, Active Patient, Upsell Abandoned and Cold.

Tags such as source-landing-page, stage-consult-booked, behavior-consult-completed, behavior-whitening-booked and status-no-show move contacts between stages automatically.

3. Automation workflows

Trigger-and-action workflows cover the whole patient journey, including the three-touch no-show recovery (1 hour, 24 hours, 72 hours).

4. Email and SMS sequences

Every message is written in Bloom Dental’s voice: professional and approachable, clean and modern, warm but confident, never overly clinical. Each one has a single clear next step, such as booking the consult or claiming a whitening slot.

5. Post-consultation whitening upsell

Only contacts whose appointment is marked Showed enter the upsell workflow. The PHP 15,000 Premium In-Chair Teeth Whitening offer reaches patients while the consultation is fresh. A click on the whitening link tags the contact with behavior-whitening-interest. A booking tags them customer-whitening-purchased and moves them to Upsell Booked.

My decisions and why

  • Free 30-minute consultation as the entry offer. It removes the barrier to a first visit for a time-poor, image-conscious audience. It also gives a natural reason to follow up.
  • Meta Ads to a dedicated landing page with trust signals. The audience discovers the clinic on Instagram and Facebook. Before-and-after photos, Google reviews and credentials answer the trust question early.
  • Structured reminders plus a three-touch recovery. A 35% no-show rate is the second biggest leak, so I tackled it twice. Reminders prevent no-shows. SMS, email and SMS at 1, 24 and 72 hours win back the ones that still happen.
  • Whitening offer triggered by a completed consult. Only patients who attended receive it, at the point where they have already experienced the clinic.
  • Tags and an eight-stage pipeline. Every contact’s status is visible at a glance, and the front desk no longer tracks it by hand.
