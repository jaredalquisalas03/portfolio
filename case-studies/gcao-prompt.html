GCAO Prompt Example

How I brief AI before any strategy doc or copy draft.

Framework
Goal, Context, Action, Output
Used for
Strategy docs, funnel plans, email and SMS copy, workflow specs
Example
Sample brief for a coaching business (not a client build)

Honesty note: This is a sample brief for a fictional coaching business. It shows the structure I use on every project. It is not one of my case study builds.

Why this format

A vague prompt gets a vague draft. GCAO forces every brief to carry the same four parts, so the output comes back usable.

  • Goal sets measurable targets, so the output is judged against numbers.
  • Context gives the business, audience, current numbers, tools and voice rules.
  • Action lists the exact steps, so nothing is skipped.
  • Output sets the format, length limits and a final compliance check.

The prompt

[GOAL]
Get Iron Path Coaching to three targets in 60 days:
(1) 50 booked Body Assessments from Meta Ads,
(2) no-shows cut from 40% to under 20%,
(3) 35% of people who attend an assessment enrolling in the 12-Week Transformation Program.
A second goal: free the owner from doing every DM, booking and follow-up by hand.

[CONTEXT]
- Business: Iron Path Coaching, a strength training and nutrition coaching business in Cebu City, Philippines.
- Offers:
  - Free 45-minute Body Assessment & Strategy Session (the entry offer)
  - 12-Week Iron Path Transformation Program, PHP 45,000 (the main upsell)
  - 1:1 in-studio coaching and online remote coaching
  - Ongoing Coaching Membership, PHP 6,000/month (recurring revenue)
- Ideal customer (use for targeting and messaging only): busy professionals aged 28-48 in or near Cebu City. They care about health and how they look. They have started and quit fitness before. They are short on time. They distrust generic PDF plans and want a real coach. They find the business through Instagram, TikTok, referrals and Meta Ads. Real client transformations and a visible coach build their trust.
- Reader of this document: the owner. They will review it and set it up personally. They run all leads and scheduling by hand today. Write for them, not for the customer.
- Current numbers: about 35 leads/month, 8 bookings (23%). No-show rate is 40%, with no recovery sequence. Nothing is in place to convert attendees after the assessment.
- Tools: Meta Ads Manager and GoHighLevel (SMS, email, WhatsApp/Messenger, calendars, pipelines, workflows, AI chat agents).
- Budget: [Meta Ads monthly budget, or "not set"]. If no budget is given, focus on structure and order of steps, not spend amounts.
- Brand voice: strong, disciplined and motivating. Never intimidating or bro-culture. Approachable, non-judgmental and never shame-based.
- Banned in ALL copy: the words "shred" and "diet", extreme before/after shaming, and claims like "X pounds in a week".

[ACTION]
1. Map the funnel: Meta Ad/DM lead → booked → attended → enrolled. Show the current conversion at each stage and the biggest drop-off.
2. Run the target math. Work out how many leads per month are needed to reach 50 bookings at a realistic booking rate. If 50 is out of reach at the current lead volume, say so plainly. Then give the lead volume or booking rate that would make it work.
3. Design lead-to-booking fixes: ad angles and creative types for this audience, lead form vs. click-to-Messenger vs. landing page, instant speed-to-lead replies, and an AI DM agent that qualifies leads and books them into the calendar.
4. Design the no-show sequence from booking to the appointment and after. For each touchpoint give the timing (e.g. "booking +0 min", "24h before"), the channel, the goal, and the message angle. Include a reschedule path and a 3-step recovery for people who miss.
5. Design the post-assessment conversion sequence. For each touchpoint give the timing after the session (e.g. "+2h", "Day 3"), the channel, the goal, and the angle. Cover proof, objection handling (price, time, past failures), a clear decision deadline, and a downsell to the Membership for people who say no to the program.
6. List every task the owner does by hand today. Next to each one, give the automation or AI step that replaces it and the estimated hours saved per week.
7. Build a 60-day rollout in weekly milestones. Tie each milestone to one of the three numeric targets, with a leading KPI to check every week.

[OUTPUT]
- A structured strategy document with these headings:
  (1) Funnel Overview & Diagnosis
  (2) Lead-to-Booking Improvements
  (3) No-Show Reduction Sequence
  (4) Post-Assessment Conversion Sequence
  (5) 60-Day Rollout Timeline
  (6) Owner Workload: Manual → Automated
- Present sequences (3) and (4) as tables with these columns: Touchpoint | Timing | Channel | Goal | Message angle | Sample copy.
- Copy limits: SMS under 160 characters, email subject under 50 characters, email body under 150 words.
- Detailed but lean. No generic marketing theory.
- Label any benchmark or estimate "estimate" or "to test". Don't present unverified industry numbers or platform features as fact.
- Before finishing, check all sample copy against the banned words and claims.

How I use it with clients

  • I fill the Context block from the discovery call, using the client’s own words and numbers.
  • I keep the structure the same for every client. Only the facts change.
  • I review every draft before it reaches the client or goes live.
