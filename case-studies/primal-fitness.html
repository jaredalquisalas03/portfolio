Primal Fitness

A lead-to-client funnel for online coaching.

Client type
Online fitness and lifestyle coaching brand, international English-speaking audience
Project type
Self-initiated portfolio project (fictional, pre-launch business, not a paid client engagement)
My role
Strategy, personas, lead magnet, funnel design, copy, automation spec, build
Built
Opt-in funnel, nurture sequence, lead qualification, no-show recovery, enrollment and onboarding, two pipelines

The problem

Primal Fitness has a clear offer but no system to turn social attention into paying clients. Four issues drove this project:

  • No list, no follow-up. Interested people see the brand, then drift away. There is no email list, and leads who download the free 7-day starter get no nurture.
  • No qualification step. Without a qualifying form, unfit leads take up strategy call slots meant for serious leads.
  • Lost leads. With no automated reminders and no no-show recovery, booked strategy calls are lost and the coach chases leads by hand.
  • No honest client definition. With no onboarding and no payment rules, a form fill or a started checkout could be mistaken for a paid client and distort the numbers.

Primal Fitness is pre-launch. The GoHighLevel build started from scratch, with no existing pipelines, tags or workflows, and no baseline data exists.

The solution

Free online fitness advice is everywhere, and the people Primal Fitness wants to coach are the ones it has overwhelmed. They have quit before and fear doing it again. I built the funnel around the primary persona, Alex, the “Restarting Beginner.” It moves people from attention to list to qualified application, with no hard sell up front.

Alex opts in for the free Primal Reset: 7-Day Fitness Starter Guide using only first name, last name and email. The guide arrives immediately by email, and the lead is saved as a contact in the CRM. A short nurture sequence answers objections. A thank-you page invites Alex to a free webinar, which points to a 1-on-1 coaching application.

A GHL workflow qualifies each applicant. Qualified leads can book a strategy call, with reminders and no-show recovery. Others stay in nurture. Only a completed payment counts as a client, and then the welcome page and onboarding begin. The goal: 10 new clients in 60 days from organic and referral traffic.

What I built

1. Landing page and funnel pages

A GHL funnel covering the opt-in page (/starter-guide), the thank-you and guide delivery page (/guide-thank-you), the coaching page (/primal-foundations), the optional qualification form (/coaching-application), checkout (/enroll) and the post-purchase page (/welcome). The thank-you page delivers the guide whether or not the lead does anything else. It also adds an optional webinar CTA and a direct coaching CTA.

2. Pipelines

I built two pipelines in GoHighLevel. The first, Primal Fitness 1-on-1 Coaching, tracks every lead from first opt-in to an enrolled client: New Lead, Application Submitted, Qualified, Call Booked, Showed, No Show and Enrolled.

The second, Primal Fitness Upsell and Retention, picks up after enrollment. It tracks each client through Active Client, Soft Upsell Sent, Program Completed and Upsell Offered.

The contact record and opportunity update at every stage. A repeat submission updates the existing record instead of creating a second opportunity.

3. Automation workflows

  • W1, Lead capture, guide delivery and nurture sequence. A form submission creates or updates the contact, records the lead source, creates the opportunity and sends the guide immediately. Over the next few days, a nurture sequence with clear CTAs follows.
  • W2, Lead qualification and booking automation. When a lead applies for 1-on-1 coaching through the form, the workflow decides if they are qualified. Qualified leads get an email with a booking CTA. Leads who are not qualified stay in nurture.
  • W3, Call no-show recovery. Leads marked “No Show” get a recovery sequence with a booking CTA.
  • W4, Enrollment and purchase confirmation. Only a verified successful payment (simulated in this portfolio) moves a contact to Enrolled and ends promotional emails.

4. Email and SMS sequences

A guide delivery email and a 4-touch nurture sequence, written in a direct, encouraging, no-shaming voice. There are no medical claims and no fixed-time promises. The set also includes an email sequence for qualified leads, a nurture sequence for leads who are not qualified, and an SMS and email sequence for “No Show” leads.

My decisions and why

  • Free Starter Guide with no hard sell. It is useful on its own, which matters for people who are wary of free advice and past failures. It also gives a natural reason to follow up.
  • Opt-in asks only name and email. Less friction at the top of the funnel, and no medical or lifestyle history is collected.
  • Nurture follows the objections in order. Consistency first, then a simple routine, then why plans fail, while introducing the coaching program.
  • Qualification is optional, not a gate. The readiness form segments leads without blocking anyone. Applications act as the signal instead of a strategy-call booking, which keeps the scope tight.
  • Only a verified payment makes a client. Form fills and abandoned checkouts never create a client, so the pipeline and the numbers stay honest.
  • Simulated payments tagged demo-contact. Test revenue stays out of reporting, so the portfolio never presents invented money as real.
  • UTM and lead source on every contact. The first real data can show which channel actually produces clients.
  • Compliance built in. No medical claims or guaranteed outcomes, flagged placeholders instead of fabricated testimonials, and consent plus an easy unsubscribe on marketing emails.
