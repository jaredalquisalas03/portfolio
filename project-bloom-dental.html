Bloom Dental Studio: An Automated Lead-to-Consultation Funnel

A GoHighLevel funnel for a Metro Manila dental clinic that takes a cold Meta Ads click to a booked free 30-minute consultation, protects the booking with automatic reminders, recovers missed appointments and offers Premium Whitening after every completed consult.

The problem

Bloom Dental Studio, led by Dr. Chloe, is a busy clinic with a leaky front end. Four issues drove this project:

  • Leads slip away before booking. About 20 website leads arrive each month, but only about 6 book an appointment, a 30% rate.
  • Manual front desk. Scheduling, confirmation calls, reminders and no-show follow-ups are all handled by hand, with no automated system behind them.
  • High no-shows. 35% of booked patients don’t attend, and there is no recovery sequence to bring them back.
  • No post-consultation follow-up. Nothing exists to turn patients who complete a consult into Premium Whitening bookings.

The solution

I designed the funnel for the primary persona, [persona name], the busy professional: aged 28 to 45, living or working in BGC or Makati, career-focused, image-conscious and short on time. Rather than asking for a big commitment, the funnel leads with a free 30-minute new-patient consultation and backs it with the trust signals this audience looks for: before-and-after photos, Google reviews and visible credentials. Booking is built to need as little back-and-forth as possible.

Once booked, the patient moves through automatic confirmation and reminders. Patients who miss the appointment get a three-touch recovery sequence. Patients who attend get a Premium Whitening offer. The front desk no longer chases any of it by hand.

What I built

1. Landing page and funnel pages

A GHL landing page with the Bloom Dental New Patient Inquiry form above the fold, a thank-you page, and a booking page for the Free 30-Min New Patient Consult calendar. [Confirm page names and whether the booking widget is embedded on the landing page or the thank-you page.]

2. Pipeline

A pipeline named Bloom Dental Consultations tracks every lead from first inquiry through to an active patient: New Lead, Booking Attempted, Consult Booked, Consult Completed, Upsell Booked, Active Patient and Cold. Tags such as source-landing-page, stage-consult-booked, behavior-consult-completed, behavior-whitening-booked and status-no-show move contacts between stages automatically.

My decisions and why

  • Free 30-minute consultation as the entry offer: it removes the barrier to a first visit for a time-poor, image-conscious audience and gives a natural reason to follow up.
  • Meta Ads to a dedicated landing page with trust signals: the audience discovers the clinic on Instagram and Facebook, and before-and-after photos, Google reviews and credentials answer the trust question early.
  • A five-email nurture focused on booking: 70% of leads are lost between inquiry and booking, so the biggest lever sits there. The sequence covers trust and pricing objections before asking one last time.
  • Structured reminders plus a three-touch recovery: a 35% no-show rate is the second biggest leak, so I tackled it twice: reminders to prevent no-shows, and SMS, email, SMS at 1, 24 and 72 hours to win back the ones that happen.
  • Whitening offer triggered by a completed consult: only patients who attended receive it, at the point where they have already experienced the clinic.
  • Tags and a seven-stage pipeline: every contact’s status is visible at a glance, and the front desk no longer tracks it by hand.
